About Rest Mode

Back to Rest Mode

Rest Mode is a review blog for people whose backlog is bigger than their free time. Between work, errands, and everything else, most of us don't have 100+ hours to sink into a single game anymore — but that doesn't mean we've stopped wanting to play.

What This Blog Is About

Every review here answers one question first: can you actually enjoy this game with limited, fragmented time? That means paying attention to session length, how forgiving a game is about picking it back up after a week away, and whether progress feels meaningful even in short bursts.

You'll still get the usual review coverage — pitch, gameplay loop, pros and cons, and a final verdict — but always filtered through the lens of "does this respect my schedule?"

The Rest Mode Rating

Each review ends with a five-star Rest Mode Rating. It isn't a measure of overall quality; a three-star game here can still be a masterpiece. It's a measure of fit for a limited-time player: five stars means you can pick it up for twenty minutes and feel satisfied, while lower scores mean the game really wants a bigger, more uninterrupted block of your time.

Who's Writing This

This blog is a side project by Felipe del Canto, written in whatever spare time is left over after research, teaching, and everything else that competes for his attention — which is, fittingly, exactly the problem this blog is about.
