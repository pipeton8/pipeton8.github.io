Welcome to The Busy Gamer

Let's face it: adulting is hard, and gaming time is precious. I review games with one question in mind: can you actually play this with limited time during the week? Each review includes session length, time-to-complete estimates, and a Busy Gamer Rating™ to help you decide if it's worth your limited gaming hours.
